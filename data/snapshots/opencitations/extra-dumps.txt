 Open Citations Meta
 The Open Citations Meta database stores and delivers bibliographic metadata for all publications involved in the Open Citations Index. 
 Most recent OpenCitations Meta data dump - June 2026 
 Released on 2026-06-27, compared to the previous version, includes metadata related to citing and cited bibliographic resources added from the February 2026 version of Crossref and the August 2025 version of JaLC . It also includes an alignment with OpenAlex identifiers and introduces OUTCITE and Matilda as new sources.

 Open Citations Index
 The Open Citations Index stores OMID-to-OMID references representing all the references gathered from several sources. 
 Most recent OpenCitations Index data dump - July 2026 
 Released on 2026-07-28, this dataset adds the citation data contained in the February 2026 version of Crossref and the August 2025 version of JaLC . Also this version introduces two new sources: OUTCITE and Matilda .
