 About J-STAGE WebAPI 
 J-STAGE WebAPI is an application programming interface (API) that enables users to retrieve metadata for J-STAGE content in XML format. 
 By implementing the J-STAGE WebAPI, users can: 
 ・ 
 Extract data from J-STAGE content 
 You can store a list of published journals, issues and tables of contents from journals on J-STAGE. The XML data can be imported into Microsoft Excel and other applications and manipulated. 
 Note that downloading a large amount of data is not permitted under the J-STAGE Browsing Policy . 
 ・ 
 Link to J-STAGE content from your own website 
 Subscribing institutions and academic societies can extract lists of titles and published issues, tables of contents and URLs of journals from J-STAGE and display them on their websites. This lets their users access J-STAGE content quickly and easily. 
 ・ 
 Perform integrated searches with other databases 
 J-STAGE WebAPI can be used in conjunction with APIs provided by JST (J-GLOBAL), the National Institute of Informatics (CiNii Articles), and the National Diet Library (NDL Search). Visit their websites to learn more about these APIs and their terms of use. 
 ・ 
 Obtain up-to-date information 
 By registering the URL of J-STAGE WebAPI in an RSS reader, you can check updated information for articles. 
 Using the J-STAGE WebAPI 
 By specifying the search criteria with parameters and accessing the URL for the request, you can obtain the search result metadata from each content type.
 Content Type 
 Overview 
 Search terms (specified by parameters) 
 Available metadata (XML format) 
 Journal issue 
 Obtain a list of issue numbers for the specified title. 
 Title, ISSN, year of publication, publication code, etc. 
 A list of volumes and issues of titles that match the search criteria. Detailed information such as publisher, ISSN, latest release date, and the URL of the table of contents can be obtained at the same time. 
 Result of article search 
 Obtain a list of the table of contents for the specified title. You can also search for articles using specific search criteria. 
 Title, ISSN, year of publication, publication code, article title, author, keywords, etc. 
 List of articles published on J-STAGE. Detailed information such as DOI, publication date, and bibliographic information can be obtained at the same time. 
 Result of journal search 
 Obtain a list of the published journals for the specified title. You can also search for journals using specific search criteria. 
 Journal title, ISSN, year of publication, publication code, publisher name, publication type, access type, language, peer review, immediate open access status, etc. 
 List of journals published on J-STAGE. Detailed information such as journal title, publisher name, publication year, ISSN, and immediate open access information can be obtained at the same time.

 Terms of use 
 In order to use the J-STAGE WebAPI, you must agree to the Terms of Use (in Japanese only) . Use of the API implies that you have agreed to the Terms of Use. 
 Please note that users take full responsibility for their own use of the J-STAGE WebAPI. JST takes no responsibility for third-party use of the API or their outcomes.
 Commercial use 
 Permission is required for commercial use of the J-STAGE WebAPI, regardless of whether the use leads to commercial gain for the user. Please download the 
 application form for using the J-STAGE WebAPI (in Japanese) , fill it out, and submit to J-STAGE at contact[at]jstage.jst.go.jp (replace [at] with @).
